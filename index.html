03 About

I'm focused on building practical IT skills through hands-on home lab projects. My approach is to take a system from concept to production — then document it thoroughly: the architecture decisions, the things that broke, and how they were fixed.

The Privacy Pi project reflects that methodology. It started as a simple Pi-hole installation and expanded through multiple phases into a layered DNS privacy stack with automated monitoring, alerting, reporting, and offsite backup — all deployed on a headless Raspberry Pi 4 serving an entire home network.

I'm particularly interested in roles that involve Linux systems administration, network infrastructure, security hardening, and the operational side of IT.
